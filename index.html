    Good to know

    How long will my appointment take?

    The total on your visit summary adds up the time for each service. Braids and colour take longer, so book a morning slot.

    Can I cancel or change my time?

    Yes. Cancel from “Your bookings” above, or message us at least a day ahead.

    Should I come with clean hair?

    For braids and locs, arrive with clean, dry, detangled hair. For cuts and colour, come as you are.

    Do you do kids’ hair?

    Yes. Pick “Kids’ cut or braids” and we’ll allow extra time.
